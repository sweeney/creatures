Summary of features: Simulates a 3 layered food chain, grass, rabbits and foxes; two real-time displays: Colour coded 2D representation of the current state of the population; traditional graph display but updated in real-time; save and load simulations, print out graphs showing changes in population levels, many configurations; Alterable real-time parameters: solar energy input, grass and rabbit death rates; fox death rate, fox hunting ability, rabbit birth rate rules; inflict preprogrammed disasters such as forest fire, asteroid collision etc.; add disease to rabbit population and trace spread visually. Some applications: study exponential grow, logistic growth, use creatures to simulate bacterial growth, predatory/prey interactions, including population oscillations, build pyramids of numbers, study importance of photosynthesis, spread of disease as a function of population density, etc. etc!

Requires minimum Windows 95. The demo, which can be downloaded below, is a full version but with only 120 seconds of running time, the program must then be restarted.
