Foxes and Rabbits Simulator · 1996

Creatures 2.0

A field of grass grows under sunlight; rabbits eat it and breed; foxes hunt the rabbits. Change the parameters and watch the populations oscillate, stabilise or collapse.

Reverse-engineered and ported to the web from the original educational simulation published in 1996 by H M Sauro.

Run the simulator How it was recovered Source

Creatures 1.0 was an educational simulation published in 1996 by H M Sauro, and in common use on school computers across the UK in the late 1990s, where I first encountered it.

It ran on 16-bit Windows and has not been usable on ordinary hardware for decades. This is the same simulation, in a browser, with the same rules — recovered directly from the original CREATURE.EXE.

The Algorithm

  • Animals move orthogonally, at most half the time. The dispatch draws a number from one to eight and acts on only four of them. There are no diagonal moves — and on an even draw, none at all.
  • A blocked move still writes the animal back. Miss that single store and the whole population quietly dies out, because the grass pass has already overwritten the cell.
  • A fox that catches a rabbit breeds into its cell. Hunting and reproduction are not separate steps.
  • Disasters change a parameter, not the field. Fire, disease and over-hunting are temporary shocks that revert on a timer. The asteroid is permanent.
  • Three of the four disasters move their slider with the wrong arithmetic — a bug in the original, harmless to the simulation and visible only in the number underneath.
